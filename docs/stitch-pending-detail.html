Wild Mushroom Risotto

Description

This creamy, comforting Wild Mushroom Risotto is packed with the deep, earthy flavors of porcini and cremini mushrooms. Slowly cooked with arborio rice and finished with fresh parmesan and butter, it's the perfect dish for a cozy evening or an elegant dinner party.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Arborio rice
  • 1 lb Mixed wild mushrooms
  • 6 cups Vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup Dry white wine
