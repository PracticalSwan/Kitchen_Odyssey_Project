Rustic Roasted Tomato Basil Soup

Sarah Jenkins

Posted 2 hours ago • 45 min prep

Account pending approval

This rustic roasted tomato basil soup is the ultimate comfort food. Made with fresh roma tomatoes, garlic, onions, and plenty of fresh basil, it's rich, creamy without heavy cream, and perfect for a chilly evening. Serve with a grilled cheese sandwich for the complete experience.

Instructions

  1. 1

    Roast the Tomatoes

    Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. Place halved tomatoes and garlic cloves on the baking sheet. Drizzle generously with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Roast for 40-45 minutes, until soft and charred.

  2. 2

    Sauté Onions

    While tomatoes are roasting, heat a tablespoon of olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and cook until translucent and golden, about 5-7 minutes. Add red pepper flakes if using.

  3. 3

    Simmer and Blend

    Add the roasted tomatoes, garlic, and any juices from the pan into the pot with onions. Pour in the vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes. Stir in fresh basil. Use an immersion blender to blend until smooth.

  4. 4

    Serve

    Season with more salt and pepper if needed. Serve hot, garnished with fresh basil leaves, a drizzle of olive oil, or croutons.
